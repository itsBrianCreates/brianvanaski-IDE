# LinkedIn Premium Pages

LinkedIn's Premium for Pages is a subscription offering targeting small business owners, designed to enhance page visibility and improve visitor conversion.

## My role

Led strategic initiatives securing executive support, developed long-term vision, designed the MVP, and championed cross-functional collaboration to ensure alignment across teams.

## Context

Small business owners represent approximately 93% of all LinkedIn company page administrators. The initiative focused on addressing three core business objectives:

- **Stand out** — Support small business differentiation from competitors
- **Convert** — Encourage prospective clients to initiate contact
- **Grow** — Enable business expansion via increased clientele and income

## Problems

Small business administrators face obstacles in differentiation and lead conversion. Research identified three primary challenges:

- **Appearance** — Dissatisfaction with page visual presentation
- **Growth** — Slow follower growth, taking 35 months to reach 100 followers
- **Leads** — Low efficacy in qualified lead generation

By aligning business objectives with identified challenges, the design challenge crystallized:

## From Vision to MVP

Following iterative design exploration, user research, and validation testing, the team established a comprehensive vision and identified four essential MVP capabilities.

**Custom Top Card** — Convert more leads with a customizable top card, featuring a custom button, spotlight, credibility proof and a Premium badge.

**Grow Your Audience** — Auto-invite anyone who engages with your Page's content and those who follow similar Pages.

**Viewer of the Day** — See who viewed your Page to identify and convert target viewers into customers.

## Unpacking the design process

### Research

LinkedIn Pages and Premium divisions conducted exploratory research on potential premium capabilities for small business owners. Findings indicated substantial enthusiasm for features supporting business growth on the platform.

> "I'd try Premium if it could help me find several recurring local clients for my business"
> — SMB Owner

> "Customizing the page is all about driving next-level interaction"
> — SMB Owner

### Key Findings

- **Better conversion on Page** — Participants indicated willingness to invest in features improving visitor-to-follower and lead conversion
- **Tools to message page visitors** — Strong interest in capabilities identifying page viewers for follower and lead development

## Results

Premium for Pages launched in private preview in early 2024, with general availability later that year.
