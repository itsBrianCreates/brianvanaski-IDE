# Mr. Funny Jokes

**An iOS app built 100% with Claude Code.**

## What is it

A dad joke app. Simple as that. You open it, you get a joke, you either laugh or groan. Both are acceptable outcomes.

New jokes delivered daily. Clean, family-friendly, and optimized for maximum eye-rolls from anyone under 18.

## How it was built

Every line of code was written with Claude Code. The UI, the data layer, the Firestore backend, the App Store submission flow -- all of it. No Xcode templates. No boilerplate starters. Just me and an AI pair programmer building an app from scratch.

It was a proving ground for what's possible when a designer builds a real, shipping product with AI tools. Turns out: a lot.

## Why dad jokes

Because the world has enough serious apps. Sometimes you just need someone to ask you if you've heard about the restaurant on the moon. Great food, no atmosphere.

Available on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mr-funny-jokes/id6755938017). Download it and prepare yourself.
