# $mart Path

**Financial literacy education for Ohio schools.**

A collaboration with the University of Cincinnati's Economics Center, instructional designers, and teachers. We created a curriculum aligned to Ohio Common Core Standards for grades K-6.


## The challenge

How might we create a digital platform to accelerate and scale financial literacy education in Ohio schools for kids and their teachers?

## What we built

A web-based platform featuring 25 interactive HTML games and over 2 hours of animated video content. $martPath provides simple, fun lessons that bring economic and financial education to life in the classroom — teaching students about personal financial responsibility like saving, prudent spending, and prioritizing needs and wants.
