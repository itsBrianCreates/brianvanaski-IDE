# LEGO Friends

**A suite of creative play experiences for young audiences.**

How might we create a suite of creative play experiences which inspire the imagination of hands-on, minds-on girls who love LEGO Friends?


## What we built

Over about three years, I developed platform strategy for a suite of creative "maker" apps — Story Maker, Art Maker, and Music Maker — launching across web, Flash, iOS, and Android, reaching millions of users worldwide.

![Suite on devices](/images/lego-friends/suite-devices.jpg)

## Story Maker

Creative storytellers (ages 6+) can compose, develop, distribute and exchange their own multimedia narratives. Drag and drop, stickers, voice recording, text, emoticons, and backgrounds.

## Music Maker

Aspiring musicians (ages 6+) can produce original compositions using pre-recorded LEGO Friends audio clips. Mix, sing, edit, and record with characters including Livi and the Heartlake City Friends.

## Art Maker

A colorful creativity app for children ages 5+. Paint, decorate, and personalize Heart Cards and artwork with details, colors, sparkles, stickers, and embellishments.
