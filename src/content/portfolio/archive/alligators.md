# Alligators of Shark River

**Award-winning STEM education meets immersive gameplay.**

How might award-winning educational video and immersive game-like experience be combined to revolutionize STEM education?

![Alligators of Shark River cover](/images/alligators/cover.jpg)

## What we built

Working with Emmy Award-winning director Patrick Green from Symbio Studios, we created an educational app for elementary and middle school students. It combines broadcast-quality video with interactive activities, emphasizing inquiry-based learning where students discover patterns and processes independently.

A high-tech and high-touch blended methodology.
