# About

I'm Brian Vanaski, a product designer with over 20 years of experience. But I'm not just about pixels and prototypes — I'm also the creator of Mr. Funny Jokes, because I believe that everyone should know at least one good joke.

![Brian and family](/images/about/family.jpg)

## It starts with family

I've shared 22 wonderful years with my wife Meg, and together, we've raised three amazing kids: Finn, Zuzu, Mabel, and our furry friend, Mitzi. They're the heart and laughter behind my journey.

## The work

I've been at LinkedIn for 9 years. Currently a Senior Principal Product Designer, which mostly means I work on hard problems with smart people and try to make complex things feel simple. A lot of my recent work is in AI — figuring out how AI features should actually work for real humans, not just demos.

About two years ago, something clicked. I started building with AI agents — not just designing for them, but using them as collaborators. Claude Code became my go-to. I now run a small team of AI digital workers that help me ship side projects, generate content, and explore ideas way faster than I could alone.

## Outside of work

I build in the open because it's more fun that way. I like shipping things, learning by doing, and not waiting for permission. Most of my side projects start as "what if..." and end up in the App Store or on GitHub.
